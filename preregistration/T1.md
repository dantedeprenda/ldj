# Pre-registration: T1 (content moderation)

Written 2026-09-28, before any T1 test item was scored by any method. It is frozen by the commit
that adds it; the GitHub push time is the time stamp. The smoke test (`configs/t1_smoke.yaml`)
scored 150 items drawn with a different subsample size; its draft questions were discarded and
its numbers are not results.

## Task

**Data.** Measuring Hate Speech (Kennedy et al.; CC-BY-4.0), loaded by `ldj.tasks.moderation`:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Comments kept | only those with >= 3 annotations |
| Subsample | seed 0, fixed: a 3,000 train pool and 2,000 test comments |
| Label | y = 1 when `hate_speech_score > 0.5` |
| Category | platform |

**Reviewer (deferral).** One real annotator per comment, drawn with seed 0. They are right when
their rating (2 = hate speech) matches y, about 79% of the time. Their rating also feeds the
consensus score, which flatters them slightly.

**Costs.** A deferral or a label costs $0.25 (45 s at $20/h; `notes/review_costs.md`). Error
prices are (c_fp, c_fn):
- **Primary:** ($1, $1).
- **Sensitivity:** ($1, $5) and ($5, $1).

## Methods (`configs/t1.yaml`, frozen)

- **Question features.** `configs/questions/t1_v1.yaml` has 13 yes/no questions, written after
  reading training comments only. They are answered as probabilities by
  `google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it` (open weights, via OpenRouter, temperature 0, structured output)
  and fed to a logistic head (C = 1).
  - **Disclosure:** the questions overlap in concept with the dataset's own survey items
    (insult, dehumanization, violence, ...). The wording is ours, and they encode general
    domain knowledge a moderation team would have.
- **LLM judge, same model:**
  - zero-shot, at every k;
  - up to 25 of the k labels as in-context examples, at k in {5, 25} only (compute).
  - The definition of hate speech is in the instruction for both.
- **Text baselines:**
  - TF-IDF + logistic regression / kNN-10;
  - bge-base + logistic regression / kNN-10;
  - bge-large + logistic regression (C tuned by CV on the labels) / kNN-10;
  - SetFit (paraphrase-mpnet-base-v2, 20 pair iterations, one epoch, at most 500 steps).
    SetFit gets no cross-fitted cost policy because of compute. It enters the AUROC comparisons
    only.
- **Reference:** base rate and category prior.

**Budgets and draws.**
- k in {5, 10, 25, 50, 100, all (3,000)}.
- Seeds 42, 999, 2024, 2025, 3407.
- Below k = 25, every draw contains at least one comment of each outcome; otherwise draws are
  uniform.

**Thresholds** come from 5-fold cross-fitted scores on the k labels only. Two rules are used:
- **empirical:** minimum estimated cost;
- **ucb:** the error counts are replaced by 90% Clopper–Pearson upper bounds.

## Hypotheses and tests (`scripts/analyze.py configs/t1.yaml`)

"Best text baseline" = the text baseline (the 7 above) with the highest mean within-platform
AUROC at k = 25. The same rule was used in the routing paper.

| # | Claim | Test | Supported if |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 (primary) | Question features beat the best text baseline at k = 25 | Within-platform AUROC difference; hierarchical paired bootstrap over seeds and test comments | 95% CI lower bound > 0 |
| H2 | Same at k = 10 | Same | Lower bound > 0 |
| H3 | Question features are cheaper than the best text baseline at k = 25 (primary prices, ucb rule) | Cost-per-item difference, same bootstrap | 95% CI upper bound < 0 |
| E1 (exploratory, two-sided) | Question features vs the zero-shot judge, at every k | Same bootstraps | No prediction; reported either way |
| E2 (exploratory) | Whether the cheapest policy defers any items, per method and k | Descriptive | No prediction |

**Negative-result rule** (`notes/project.md`). If H1 fails, the benchmark reports that simple
methods match question features on moderation. If the zero-shot judge beats every labeled method
at every k, that is the headline for T1.

## Reporting rules

- **Failed LLM calls** are counted: neutral 0.5 at test, dropped from training. If more than 2%
  of calls fail, that is reported next to the results.
- **Spend cap:** $25 (`max_usd`).
- **The author hand-audits 200 test comments,** drawn with seed 7, before the results are
  written up. The comments are labeled blind to every method's scores. Results are reported on
  the full test set, and also with the comments the author marks as mislabeled removed, as a
  sensitivity check.
- **Any departure from this document** is reported as a deviation, with its reason.
