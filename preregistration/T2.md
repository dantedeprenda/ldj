# Pre-registration: T2 (systematic-review screening)

Written 2026-09-28, before any record of a T2 test review was scored by any method. It is frozen
by the commit that adds it; the GitHub push time is the time stamp. The smoke test
(`configs/t2_smoke.yaml`) scored subsampled records from 3 test reviews with draft questions. Its
numbers are not results. It used a different subsample: all included records kept, at most 150
per review.

## Task

**Data.** SYNERGY+ v3.0 (ASReview), loaded by `ldj.tasks.screening`:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Reviews | the official *test* reviews with >= 20 positives: 15 reviews |
| Cap | reviews over 2,000 records are subsampled uniformly to 2,000 (seed 0; prevalence kept) |
| Size | 15,121 records, 6.4% positive |
| Item | title + abstract, cut to 3,000 characters |
| Label | y = abstract-stage inclusion where the review gives it for every record (11 reviews), else final inclusion (4 reviews) |

**Split.** Inside each review, records are split 50/50 into train pool and test, stratified by y
(seed 0).

**Per-review fitting.** Every method is fit separately inside each review: its own k labels, its
own thresholds. This is how screening is done. Metrics pool the 15 reviews' test records.

**Reviewer (deferral).** A single human screener:
- sensitivity 0.866 and specificity 0.792 (Gartlehner et al. 2020);
- GBP 1.175 per record (Shemilt et al. 2016), at 1.56 USD/GBP = $1.83;
- one label costs the same.

**Context.** Every LLM prompt includes the review's research question and eligibility criteria.

## Methods (`configs/t2.yaml`, frozen)

- **Question features.** `configs/questions/t2_v1.yaml` has 12 generic yes/no questions, judged
  against the review's criteria. They were written after reading records from *training* reviews
  only. The answerer is `google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it` and the head is a logistic regression.
- **LLM judge, same model:** zero-shot only. The few-shot judge and SetFit are excluded for
  compute: every review × k × seed would need its own run.
- **Text baselines:** TF-IDF, bge-base and bge-large (C tuned), each with logistic regression
  and kNN-10.
- **Reference:** base rate.

**Budgets and draws.**
- k in {5, 10, 25, 50, 100, all} labels per review.
- Seeds 42, 999, 2024, 2025, 3407.
- **Every labeled set holds at least one included record, at every k.** Screening tools
  conventionally start from at least one known relevant record.

**Screening policy.** Auto-reject the lowest-scored records and send the rest to the screener,
so that at least 95% of included records are kept. Two thresholds are set from cross-fitted
scores on the k labels:
- **empirical:** allow as many misses as the labels permit;
- **ucb:** allow misses only if the 90% lower confidence bound on recall stays >= 95%.

Cost policies at (c_fp, c_fn) = ($1.83, $20) and ($1.83, $100) are also reported.

## Hypotheses and tests

"Best text baseline" = the one of the 6 with the highest mean within-review AUROC at k = 25.

| # | Claim | Test | Supported if |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 (primary) | Question features beat the best text baseline at k = 25 | Within-review AUROC difference; hierarchical paired bootstrap | 95% CI lower bound > 0 |
| H2 | Same at k = 10 | Same | Lower bound > 0 |
| H3 | Question features have higher WSS@95 than the best text baseline at k = 25 | Mean over seeds of review-weighted WSS@95 | Higher in at least 4 of 5 seeds |
| E1 (exploratory, two-sided) | Question features vs the zero-shot judge | Same bootstraps | No prediction |
| E2 (exploratory) | Work saved and machine recall achieved by thresholds set from k labels, both rules | Descriptive | No prediction. We expect the ucb rule to save no work below about 45 labeled positives |

**Negative-result rule.** As in T1. A zero-shot judge that beats labeled methods is reported as
the headline if it happens.

## Reporting rules

- **Failures and cap.** Failed calls are handled as in T1. Spend cap: $25.
- **Hand audit.** The author hand-audits 200 test records (seed 7, drawn across reviews), blind to
  scores. Sensitivity analysis as in T1.
- **Abstracts.** SYNERGY abstracts are not published as plain text in any output.
- **Deviations** are reported with their reasons.
