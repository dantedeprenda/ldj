What the guarantee means

Over many independent random audit samples, the chance that this procedure issues an unsafe certificate (one whose true error misses the target) is at most 5%. Audits that issue nothing count as safe, so this is not a promise about the share of issued certificates: when certificates are rare, a larger share of the issued ones can be unsafe. It says nothing about any single automated item, and it stops applying when the data source changes.

Assumptions this tool cannot check

  • The audit rows are a random sample of the items the policy will face.
  • The decision system (model, prompt, head) was frozen before the audit rows were labeled, and not changed after seeing them.
  • Deploy items come from the same distribution as audited items; a changed source needs a new audit.
  • Audit labels are the reference the target is stated against (e.g. one reviewer vs a consensus changes what is certified).
